02 / The commercial objective

Qualified conversations. Better progression. Sustainable franchise growth.

A lead is not a franchise agreement. I would align marketing, franchise development and reporting around the quality of prospects entering the pipeline and their progression through it.

01

Attract

Reach audiences actively evaluating business ownership, with brand-specific search, social and educational content.

02

Qualify

Agree criteria with sales: preferred territory, investment readiness, timeframe, relevant experience and motivation.

03

Nurture

Address barriers with approved FAQs, discovery events, franchisee stories and consent-based email sequences.

04

Convert

Measure adviser-accepted leads, discovery calls, applications and eventual agreements — not form fills alone.

Prioritisation principle

Start with the brand and territory combinations that have both recruitment demand and a credible route to conversion. Confirm priorities with the franchise development team before allocating budgets.

03 / Brand-specific approach

One framework. Three different recruitment propositions.

Mail Boxes Etc.

Hypothesis: Prospects attracted to operating a local service-led business.

Test: Local business ownership content, discovery webinars and business-centre franchise search themes.

Measure: Qualified enquiries and meeting rate by target territory.

World Options

Hypothesis: Commercially minded prospects interested in B2B services and relationship building.

Test: B2B franchise search themes, business-development content and relevant audience segments.

Measure: Sales-accepted enquiries and application progression.

PACK & SEND

Hypothesis: Prospects interested in a specialist, service-focused franchise opportunity.

Test: Specialist packing and shipping ownership content, proof-led landing pages and territory-focused campaigns.

Measure: Territory-fit rate and cost per qualified enquiry.

These are testable audience hypotheses, not claims about actual franchisee profiles or approved brand positioning.

04 / Acquisition: Google Ads + SEO

Capture genuine franchise ownership intent.

Focus on attracting people considering business ownership — not customers searching for parcel tracking, postage quotes or jobs. Test one priority brand or territory before scaling.

Paid search: intent first

Separate franchise recruitment campaigns by brand and relevant geography. Start with closely themed keywords, review search terms and exclude consumer shipping and employment intent.

Landing pages: quality first

Match each ad to approved brand information, a clear next step and proportionate qualification questions. Test cost per adviser-accepted enquiry and enquiry-to-meeting rate, not form submissions alone.

Organic search: evidence first

Use Semrush, Search Console and GA4 — subject to access — to validate technical issues, franchise-intent keywords and competitor content gaps. Prioritise three high-intent content briefs over a broad speculative content programme.

Commercial feedback loop

Connect campaign source and brand to CRM milestones where technically and legally appropriate. Review search terms, accepted-lead feedback and conversion quality with franchise development each week.

First controlled experiment

Hypothesis: A brand-specific franchise landing page with clearer investment-readiness and territory questions improves adviser acceptance, even if the raw enquiry count falls.

Success measures: qualified enquiry rate, cost per qualified enquiry, adviser acceptance and meeting progression. Agree the baseline and minimum evidence threshold before launch.

This is a proposed test, not a claim to have audited Fortidia’s Google Ads, Semrush or analytics accounts.

05 / Lead quality, CRM & measurement

Turn form submissions into qualified conversations.

Define lead quality jointly with franchise development, then track what happens after the enquiry. A low cost per lead is not a success if advisers cannot progress those prospects.

Capture useful signals

Ask for brand interest, preferred territory, available personal investment capital, investment timeframe and relevant motivation. Keep the form accessible and proportionate, with clear privacy information.

Apply agreed criteria

Use brand-specific, UK-appropriate investment requirements confirmed by the team. Available capital is only one signal: do not automatically reject a prospect based on an illustrative threshold.

Route and nurture

Assign an adviser and response expectation to promising enquiries. Route uncertain territory or financial questions for human review. Offer consent-based educational nurture to earlier-stage prospects.

Make progress visible

Use the existing CRM — HubSpot if appropriate — to capture UTMs, brand, lead owner, stage changes and adviser feedback. Audit existing licences and data quality before proposing new tools.

06 / Smarter marketing through automation

Automate the repetitive. Keep the commercial judgement.

With access to Codex, ChatGPT and/or Claude Code, I would explore targeted automation opportunities to reduce manual administration, improve franchise lead response times and give the team better visibility of recruitment performance — prioritised by business impact, implementation effort, data availability and compliance.

Competitor advertising intelligence

Use the LinkedIn Ad Library and Google Ads Transparency Center as part of competitor research to review publicly visible franchise recruitment ads, messaging and creative. Combine these observations with other permitted sources to identify positioning trends and opportunities for Fortidia’s brands.

Franchise lead qualification & routing

Identify prospect readiness using brand-approved criteria, then route enquiries to the right franchise development contact for timely follow-up.

AI-assisted enquiry responses

Support prompt, brand-appropriate acknowledgements, with human approval for investment, financial and territory-specific questions.

SEO & competitor monitoring

Track franchise-intent search opportunities, relevant content gaps and material changes in competitor visibility.

CRM pipeline alerts

Surface enquiries awaiting contact, stalled opportunities and shifts in lead quality so the team can act sooner.

How I would prioritise

Start with a small, measurable pilot using Fortidia’s approved systems and processes. Evaluate time saved, lead response speed, adviser acceptance and progression before investing in wider automation.

Specific workflows, integrations and implementation plans would be developed with the Fortidia team after reviewing existing systems, data permissions and commercial priorities. Tools such as Codex can support code development, but scheduled operation requires appropriate hosting and platform-compliant access.
