01 / The commercial objective

Qualified conversations. Better progression. Sustainable franchise growth.

A lead is not a franchise agreement. I would align marketing, franchise development and reporting around the quality of prospects entering the pipeline and their progression through it.

01

Attract

Reach audiences actively evaluating business ownership, with brand-specific search, social and educational content.

02

Qualify

Agree criteria with sales: preferred territory, investment readiness, timeframe, relevant experience and motivation.

03

Nurture

Address barriers with approved FAQs, discovery events, franchisee stories and consent-based email sequences.

04

Convert

Measure adviser-accepted leads, discovery calls, applications and eventual agreements — not form fills alone.

Prioritisation principle

Start with the brand and territory combinations that have both recruitment demand and a credible route to conversion. Confirm priorities with the franchise development team before allocating budgets.

02 / Brand-specific approach

One framework. Three different recruitment propositions.

Mail Boxes Etc.

Hypothesis: Prospects attracted to operating a local service-led business.

Test: Local business ownership content, discovery webinars and business-centre franchise search themes.

Measure: Qualified enquiries and meeting rate by target territory.

World Options

Hypothesis: Commercially minded prospects interested in B2B services and relationship building.

Test: B2B franchise search themes, business-development content and relevant audience segments.

Measure: Sales-accepted enquiries and application progression.

PACK & SEND

Hypothesis: Prospects interested in a specialist, service-focused franchise opportunity.

Test: Specialist packing and shipping ownership content, proof-led landing pages and territory-focused campaigns.

Measure: Territory-fit rate and cost per qualified enquiry.

These are testable audience hypotheses, not claims about actual franchisee profiles or approved brand positioning.

03 / Google Ads playbook

Capture intent — then optimise for quality.

Build tightly themed recruitment campaigns rather than mixing consumer shipping searches with franchise investment intent.

Campaign structure

  • Separate brand-level franchise recruitment campaigns.
  • Group keywords by business ownership, franchise opportunities and relevant geography.
  • Use phrase and exact match initially to control intent.

Protect spend

  • Review search terms and add negatives for parcel tracking, postage quotes, jobs and customer service.
  • Keep consumer service campaigns separate from franchise recruitment.
  • Use location settings appropriate to recruitment eligibility.

Improve landing pages

  • Match ad promise to each brand's approved opportunity information.
  • Use a clear enquiry CTA, FAQs and transparent next steps.
  • Test shorter forms against qualification quality, not only submission rate.

Close the loop

  • Track qualified lead events with Google Ads and GA4.
  • Import CRM milestones where technically and legally appropriate.
  • Optimise bidding to qualified outcomes once data volume is sufficient.

First test: quality before scale

Hypothesis: A brand-specific landing page with an investment-readiness question may reduce unqualified form fills while improving adviser acceptance. Run a controlled test with agreed success criteria: qualified enquiry rate, cost per qualified enquiry and enquiry-to-meeting rate.

04 / SEO & Semrush audit

Use evidence to find organic recruitment opportunities.

With Semrush access, I would conduct a practical audit before recommending content investment. This section describes the workflow, not fabricated audit results.

1. Technical health

Use Semrush Site Audit to check crawlability, indexing signals, broken links, redirects, canonicals, mobile issues and Core Web Vitals opportunities. Verify priority issues in Search Console.

2. Recruitment keywords

Use Keyword Magic Tool and Keyword Gap to group genuine franchise ownership intent by brand, location and decision stage. Exclude consumer shipping intent.

3. Competitor opportunity

Compare relevant franchise recruitment domains and landing pages, assessing search visibility, content gaps and backlink quality rather than relying on a single authority metric.

4. Conversion content

Map approved investment FAQs, support explanations, ownership guides, franchisee experiences and next-step pages to actual prospect questions.

06 / Lead quality and investment readiness

Prioritise suitable franchise prospects — not just more enquiries.

Investment capacity is one useful qualification signal, alongside territory, timeframe, motivation and the requirements of each franchise brand. I would agree qualification criteria with franchise development and measure progression, not simply form volume.

Ask the right questions

Capture available personal investment capital, preferred location and expected timeframe with clear wording, accessible forms and appropriate privacy notices.

Route intelligently

Flag enquiries for adviser review where financial readiness, territory and timing appear aligned. Offer helpful, consent-based nurture to earlier-stage prospects.

Optimise for outcomes

Compare campaigns using cost per qualified enquiry, adviser acceptance, discovery meetings and application progression — not only cost per lead.
